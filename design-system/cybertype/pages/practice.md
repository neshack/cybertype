# Practice — surface brief

- **Scope:** the single practice workspace (top bar, learning path, prompt, keyboard, session/settings, result dialogs).
- **Visitor mode:** Operate. A student completes short typing exercises; a teacher can reset progress without a manual.
- **Audience and scene:** Spanish-speaking students, roughly 8–16, on shared school netbooks or a family computer. Often offline, often 1024×600, sometimes with a touch screen.
- **Job:** identify the next key and finger, type it, understand the feedback, finish the exercise and know the next step.
- **Constraints:** no network, no accounts, no build step for the user; local progress; WCAG AA; Spanish content; physical keyboard first, touch fallback.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A tactile keycap desk that makes the next key physically obvious, refusing the generic gradient dashboard.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm paper, deep ink, indigo primary and burnt-orange accent; chunky keycaps with finger-color edges; Fredoka/Nunito; the same desk after dark in Arcade, with cyan and amber.

**STORY:** The student sees the next character, the finger and the hand; types; gets a sound, a color, a short coach line and a score; finishes with stars and one clear next action.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Brand and mode bar, the five-step learning path, the prompt tape, the finger cue and the coach. On 1440 px the keyboard and hands follow immediately; on a 600 px netbook the prompt remains the hero and the keyboard starts at the fold.

**FORM:** Code-led. No generated comp; the composition is the measured product workspace above.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
